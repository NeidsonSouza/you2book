# Product Overview

You2Book is a YouTube channel management and video aggregation application. Users can add YouTube channels by URL, and the app automatically fetches and stores all videos from those channels. The application provides a personalized dashboard where authenticated users can view their channels, browse videos, and manage their content collections.

Key features:
- User authentication with email login
- Add YouTube channels via URL (supports multiple URL formats: /channel/, /@handle, /c/custom, /user/)
- Automatic video fetching and storage from YouTube channels
- Channel and video management with owner-based authorization
- Video metadata storage (title, description, duration)
- Future capability for organizing videos into book groups and generating ebooks

The app is designed as a multi-tenant SaaS where each user has isolated access to their own channels and videos.
